# ContentLock Extension — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026_

ContentLock is a content-filtering browser extension. This policy explains what it does with data.

## What the extension does

- It blocks or redirects web pages according to a list of site rules and keywords.
- The site rules and keywords are supplied locally by the ContentLock application on the same
  computer, through the browser's managed-storage (enterprise policy) mechanism. They are not
  downloaded from any server.
- To enforce keyword rules, the extension reads the text of pages you visit **locally, in your
  browser**, to check whether a blocked word is present. If a match is found, the page is
  redirected to a local "blocked" page bundled with the extension.

## Data collection and transmission

- The extension does **not** collect, store, or transmit your browsing history, page contents,
  search queries, or any personal information.
- Page text is examined transiently in memory on your own device for the sole purpose of keyword
  matching and is never sent anywhere.
- The extension contacts **no external servers** and includes no analytics, advertising, or
  tracking.

## Permissions

- **declarativeNetRequest** — to block or redirect sites that match the configured rules.
- **storage (managed)** — to read the rules and keywords provided locally by the ContentLock
  application.
- **Host access (all sites)** — so the keyword check can run on the pages you choose to visit.

## Data sharing

None. No data is shared with the developer or any third party because no data leaves your device.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the operator of the ContentLock installation on your
device.
